# Platform Licenses & Compliance

This document outlines the licenses and registrations required for **BellyBento** as a technology platform/aggregator.

> **Important**: BellyBento is a **mediator/aggregator** - we do NOT cook, prepare, or handle food. We provide technology to connect tiffin providers with customers.

---

## Required Licenses & Registrations

| License/Registration | Authority | Purpose | Est. Cost | Timeline |
|---------------------|-----------|---------|-----------|----------|
| **Company Registration** | MCA (Ministry of Corporate Affairs) | Legal entity formation (Pvt Ltd recommended) | ₹7,000 - ₹15,000 | 7-15 days |
| **GST Registration** | GST Portal | Tax compliance for services | Free | 3-7 days |
| **FSSAI License (State/Central)** | FSSAI | Required for e-commerce food platforms | ₹2,000 - ₹5,000/year | 30-60 days |
| **Shop & Establishment Act** | Local Municipal Corporation | Business operation permit | ₹500 - ₹2,000 | 15-30 days |
| **Professional Tax** | State Government | Employee tax (if applicable) | Varies by state | 7-15 days |
| **Udyam Registration (MSME)** | MSME Ministry | Startup benefits & subsidies | Free | Instant |

---

## 1. Company Registration (Private Limited)

### Why Pvt Ltd?
- Limited liability protection
- Easier to raise investment
- Professional credibility
- Separate legal entity

### Process
1. Obtain Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)
2. Apply for Director Identification Number (DIN)
3. Reserve company name on MCA portal
4. File incorporation documents (SPICe+ form)
5. Receive Certificate of Incorporation

### Documents Required
- Aadhaar & PAN of directors
- Address proof of registered office
- Passport size photos
- Utility bill for office address

---

## 2. GST Registration

### When Required
- Mandatory if annual turnover > ₹20 lakh (services)
- Required for interstate services
- Needed for payment gateway integration

### Process
1. Visit [GST Portal](https://www.gst.gov.in)
2. Fill GST REG-01 form
3. Upload documents
4. Verify with OTP/DSC
5. Receive GSTIN

---

## 3. FSSAI License for E-commerce FBO

### Why We Need It
As per **FSSAI Guidelines for E-commerce Food Business Operators**, platforms facilitating food ordering must obtain FSSAI license.

### Our Obligations
- Obtain FSSAI license (State or Central based on operations)
- Verify all listed providers have valid FSSAI registration
- Display provider's FSSAI license number on the platform
- Responsible for grievance redressal
- Maintain records of all registered providers

### License Type

| Operations | License Type | Fee |
|------------|--------------|-----|
| Single state | State FSSAI License | ₹2,000/year |
| Multiple states | Central FSSAI License | ₹5,000/year |

### Application Process
1. Visit [FoSCoS Portal](https://foscos.fssai.gov.in)
2. Select "License" application
3. Fill Form B with business details
4. Upload required documents
5. Pay fee online
6. Schedule inspection (if required)
7. Receive license (valid for 1-5 years)

### Reference
[FSSAI E-commerce Guidelines](https://www.fssai.gov.in/cms/e-commerce.php)

---

## 4. Shop & Establishment Act

### Purpose
- Legal permission to operate business from registered address
- Employee welfare compliance

### Process
Varies by state. Generally:
1. Apply to local Municipal Corporation
2. Submit business details and address proof
3. Pay applicable fee
4. Receive registration certificate

---

## 5. Udyam Registration (MSME)

### Benefits
- Priority sector lending
- Lower interest rates
- Government tender preferences
- Subsidy schemes eligibility
- Protection against delayed payments

### Process
1. Visit [Udyam Portal](https://udyamregistration.gov.in)
2. Enter Aadhaar number
3. Fill business details
4. Self-declare investment and turnover
5. Receive Udyam Registration Number (instant)

**Cost**: Free
