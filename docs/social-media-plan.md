# BellyBento Social Media Content Plan

## Strategy: Building in Public + Storytelling

**Goal:** Create emotional investment so followers feel part of the journey and look forward to launch.

**Target:** All India (waitlist) | First Launch: Udaipur

---

## The Narrative Arc

```
Week 1: THE PROBLEM (Relatable to all India)
    ↓
Week 2: THE IDEA + WHY HOMETOWN (Personal story)
    ↓
Week 3-4: THE JOURNEY (Building updates)
    ↓
Week 5-6: THE PEOPLE (Home cooks, early users)
    ↓
Launch: UDAIPUR FIRST (Expansion teaser)
```

## Platform-Specific Guidelines

### Instagram
- Primary platform for visual content
- Use carousel for story posts
- Stories for daily micro-updates
- Reels for behind-the-scenes
- Save chapters as Highlights

### Facebook
- Cross-post from Instagram
- Longer captions OK
- Good for community building
- Share in relevant local groups

### X (Twitter)
- Shorter, punchier versions
- Thread format for longer stories
- Good for founder/startup community
- Engage with #BuildingInPublic community

### LinkedIn
- Professional, founder-focused content
- Business angle: market opportunity, startup journey
- Great for investor visibility and partnerships
- Use personal stories with professional insight
- Engage with startup/entrepreneur community
- Profile: linkedin.com/company/bellybento/
