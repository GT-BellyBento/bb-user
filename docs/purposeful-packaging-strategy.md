## Food Safety Considerations

### ⚠️ CRITICAL: Food Must Never Be Compromised

This section addresses the concern: **Can food affect packaging or vice versa?**

---

### Risk Analysis

| Risk | Description | Severity |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **Moisture activation** | Liquid foods (dal, sabzi) could trigger seed germination | High |
| **Taste contamination** | Seeds/seed coating might affect food taste | High |
| **Chemical leaching** | Seed treatments might leach into food | High |
| **Structural failure** | Wet food might degrade packaging faster | Medium |
| **Allergies** | Some seeds can trigger allergies | Medium |

---

### Expert Guidance: The Golden Rule

> **NEVER put seeds in direct contact with food.**

```
❌ WRONG: Seeds embedded in food container
   └── Food moisture activates seeds
   └── Seeds might affect taste
   └── Chemical concerns

✅ RIGHT: Seeds in separate insert/bag
   └── No food contact
   └── No moisture issue
   └── Safe and effective
```

---

### Food-Safe Packaging Layers

**Recommended Structure:**

```
LAYER 1: Food Container (Inner)
├── Material: Sugarcane bagasse / Kraft paper
├── Properties: Food-safe, grease-resistant
├── Coating: Natural wax or water-based coating
├── Seeds: ❌ NONE
└── Purpose: Hold food safely

LAYER 2: Container Lid
├── Material: Bagasse / Paper
├── Seeds: ❌ NONE
└── Purpose: Seal food

LAYER 3: Seed Paper Card (Separate)
├── Material: Recycled paper with embedded seeds
├── Seeds: ✅ YES (Tulsi, Coriander, Methi)
├── Placement: Inside carry bag, NOT touching food
└── Purpose: Plantable insert

LAYER 4: Carry Bag (Outer)
├── Material: Option A - Regular paper bag
│            Option B - Seed paper bag
├── Seeds: Optional (doesn't touch food)
└── Purpose: Carry containers
```

---

### Food-Specific Concerns

| Food Type | Concern | Solution |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| **Liquid dal/sabzi** | Moisture, spills | Leak-proof container with tight lid |
| **Oily foods** | Grease seepage | Grease-resistant coating on container |
| **Hot foods** | Heat + moisture | Ventilation holes, quality bagasse |
| **Acidic foods** | May degrade paper faster | Food-grade coating |
| **Rice/roti (dry)** | Minimal concern | Standard packaging works |

---

### Coating & Treatment Guidelines

**For Food-Contact Containers:**

| Coating Type | Food Safe? | Properties |
|--------------|------------|------------|
| **PLA (Polylactic Acid)** | ✅ Yes | Biodegradable, good moisture barrier |
| **Natural wax (beeswax)** | ✅ Yes | Traditional, eco-friendly |
| **Water-based coating** | ✅ Yes | Grease-resistant |
| **PFAS/Teflon** | ❌ No | Harmful, avoid |
| **Plastic lining** | ❌ No | Not eco-friendly |

**For Seed Paper (Non-Food Contact):**

| Requirement | Standard |
|-------------|----------|
| Seeds | Untreated, organic preferred |
| Paper | Recycled, chemical-free |
| Ink | Soy-based or vegetable ink |
| Adhesives | Water-based, non-toxic |

---

### Allergy Considerations

| Seed | Allergy Risk | Recommendation |
|------|--------------|----------------|
| **Tulsi (Basil)** | Very low | ✅ Safe for most |
| **Coriander** | Low | ✅ Safe for most |
| **Methi (Fenugreek)** | Low | ✅ Safe for most |
| **Marigold** | Very low | ✅ Safe (flower, not food) |
| **Mustard** | Medium | ⚠️ Avoid (common allergen) |
| **Sesame** | High | ❌ Avoid (major allergen) |

**Recommendation:** Stick to Tulsi, Coriander, Methi, Marigold - all low allergy risk.

---

### Testing & Certification

Before launching, ensure:

| Test | Purpose | Who Does It |
|------|---------|-------------|
| **FSSAI compliance** | Food container safety | Certified lab |
| **Migration testing** | Check if chemicals leach into food | Certified lab |
| **Seed viability test** | Seeds actually grow | In-house |
| **Moisture resistance** | Container doesn't leak | Supplier + in-house |
| **Grease resistance** | Oil doesn't seep through | Supplier + in-house |

## FAQs for Customer Support

**Q: Will the seeds in my packaging affect my food?**
> A: No! The seed paper card is placed separately in the carry bag and never touches your food. Your food containers are 100% food-safe with no seeds.

**Q: What if I don't want to plant it?**
> A: No problem! The card is 100% compostable. You can throw it in your compost, garden, or regular waste - it will decompose naturally.

**Q: My plant didn't grow. What happened?**
> A: Seeds need proper care - adequate sunlight, water, and soil. Try again with our tips: [link]. If multiple cards fail, contact us for replacements.

**Q: Are the seeds organic?**
> A: We use untreated, chemical-free seeds suitable for home gardening.

**Q: Can I choose which seed I get?**
> A: Coming soon! We're working on letting you select your preferred seed type in the app.
