A practical guide for when and how to raise investment for BellyBento.

## 1. Should You Go to Investors?

### Recommendation: **NOT YET**

### Why Wait?

1. **Valuation will be MUCH higher** with traction - You'll give away less equity
2. **More negotiating power** - "We're growing" vs "We have an idea"
3. **Investors say no to ideas** - They say yes to businesses
4. **You'll learn what users actually want** before burning investor money

### Expert Recommendation

**Bootstrap to MVP + 50-100 paying customers first.** Then raise.

---

## 2. How Much to Ask & For What Equity

### If You Raise NOW (Pre-MVP) - Not Recommended

| Ask | Equity | Valuation |
|-----|--------|-----------|
| ₹10-25 lakhs | 15-25% | ₹40L-1Cr |

**Reality:** Pre-revenue startups in India get painful terms.

### If You Raise AFTER Traction (Recommended)

| Traction Level | Ask | Equity | Valuation |
|----------------|-----|--------|-----------|
| 100 paying customers, ₹50K MRR | ₹25-50 lakhs | 10-15% | ₹2.5-5 Cr |
| 500 customers, ₹2L MRR | ₹50L-1 Cr | 10-15% | ₹5-10 Cr |

### Golden Rules

- Never give more than 15-20% in early rounds
- Always keep founder majority (51%+) until Series A
- Reserve 10-15% for ESOP (employee stock options)

---

## 3. Equity vs Debt vs Royalty

| Type | How It Works | Best For | Risk |
|------|--------------|----------|------|
| **Equity** | Give ownership % | High-growth startups | Dilution, loss of control |
| **Debt** | Loan with interest | Stable businesses with revenue | Must repay even if failing |
| **Revenue Royalty** | Pay % of revenue until X amount | Lifestyle businesses | Ongoing cash drain |

### For BellyBento: **Equity** (when you raise)

**Why:**
- Marketplace = high growth potential
- No revenue yet = can't service debt
- Royalty doesn't make sense for tech startups

## 4. Is BellyBento Worth Investment?

### ✅ Yes, the IDEA is investable

| Factor | BellyBento |
|--------|------------|
| **Large Market** | ₹50,000 Cr+ food delivery market in India |
| **Real Problem** | 40M+ people living away from home |
| **Clear Revenue Model** | Commission + subscriptions |
| **Network Effects** | More cooks → more customers → more cooks |
| **Timing** | Post-COVID homemade food preference |

### ⚠️ Common Investor Questions (Prepare Answers)

1. "Why will this work when others failed?" (HomeFoodi, Mealful, etc.)
2. "How will you acquire customers cheaply?"
3. "How will you ensure quality control?"
4. "What's your unfair advantage?"
5. "Why are YOU the right person to build this?"
6. "What happens if Swiggy/Zomato enters this space?"

## 5. What to Prepare Before Going to Investors

### Document Checklist

| Document | Priority | When to Prepare |
|----------|----------|-----------------|
| Working MVP | 🔴 Critical | Before anything else |
| Traction Data | 🔴 Critical | After MVP launch |
| Company Registration (Pvt Ltd) | 🔴 High | Before raising |
| Pitch Deck (10-15 slides) | 🔴 High | After getting traction |
| Financial Projections (3-5 years) | 🟡 Medium | Before investor meetings |
| Cap Table | 🟡 Medium | Before term sheet |
| Founder Agreement | 🟡 Medium | If multiple founders |
| Business Plan | 🟢 Low | For due diligence |

### The Right Order of Preparation

```
1. Build MVP (even basic version)
        ↓
2. Launch in Udaipur (10 cooks, 50 customers)
        ↓
3. Get paying customers (prove unit economics)
        ↓
4. Collect data (retention, revenue, CAC)
        ↓
5. Register company (Pvt Ltd)
        ↓
6. Prepare pitch deck with REAL numbers
        ↓
7. Approach investors
```

## 6. Bootstrap Plan (Recommended Path)

### What to Do Instead of Raising Now

| Action | Timeline | Estimated Cost |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| Build basic MVP (web app) | 4-6 weeks | ₹0 (self-built) |
| Launch in Udaipur | 2 weeks | ₹10-20K (marketing) |
| Onboard 10 home cooks/tiffin services | 2-4 weeks | Hustle + ₹5K |
| Acquire 50 paying customers | 4-8 weeks | ₹15-20K (ads/referrals) |
| Prove retention & unit economics | 2-3 months | Operations cost |

**Total Bootstrap Budget:** ₹50K - ₹1L

### Benefits of Bootstrapping First

1. **Better valuation** - 5-10x higher with traction
2. **Less dilution** - Give away 10-15% instead of 20-25%
3. **More investor interest** - Traction = proof = confidence
4. **Learn faster** - Real users teach you what to build
5. **Negotiate from strength** - "We're growing" vs "We need money"

## 8. Startup India Seed Fund Scheme

### Overview

Government initiative providing up to ₹25 lakhs to early-stage startups.

### Eligibility

- Registered as Pvt Ltd / LLP
- Less than 2 years old
- Not received more than ₹10L funding
- Working on innovative/scalable product

### How to Apply

1. Register on Startup India portal
2. Get DPIIT recognition
3. Apply through empaneled incubators
4. Submit business plan + pitch

### Link
[Startup India Seed Fund](https://seedfund.startupindia.gov.in/)

## 11. Key Metrics Investors Will Ask

### Must-Know Numbers

| Metric | What It Means | Target |
|--------|---------------|--------|
| **MRR** | Monthly Recurring Revenue | ₹50K+ before raising |
| **CAC** | Cost to Acquire Customer | < ₹500 |
| **LTV** | Lifetime Value of Customer | > 3x CAC |
| **Take Rate** | % commission per order | 10-15% |
| **Retention** | % customers staying monthly | > 70% |
| **GMV** | Total transaction value | Shows scale |

### Formula Reference

```
CAC = Total Marketing Spend / New Customers Acquired
LTV = Avg Order Value × Orders/Month × Customer Lifetime (months)
Take Rate = Platform Earnings / Total Transaction Value × 100
LTV:CAC Ratio = LTV / CAC (should be > 3)
```

## 13. Red Flags to Avoid

### What Kills Investor Interest

| Red Flag | Why It's Bad |
|----------|--------------|
| No traction, just idea | Shows you can't execute |
| Unrealistic projections | ₹100 Cr revenue in Year 1 = not credible |
| Unclear business model | "We'll figure out monetization later" |
| No differentiation | "We're like Swiggy but for tiffins" |
| Asking for too much | ₹5 Cr for a pre-MVP startup |
| Giving away too much equity | 50% for seed round = bad signal |
| Solo founder with no tech skills | Who will build it? |

## 14. If You MUST Raise Now

### Best Options for Pre-Traction

1. **Friends & Family Round**
   - Amount: ₹2-5L
   - Terms: Informal or convertible note
   - Pros: Quick, no pitch needed
   - Cons: Mixing money with relationships

2. **Angel Investors**
   - Amount: ₹10-25L
   - Terms: 15-25% equity
   - Where: AngelList India, LetsVenture, Indian Angel Network
   - Pros: Mentorship + network
   - Cons: High dilution at low valuation

3. **Incubator Programs**
   - Amount: ₹5-15L + support
   - Terms: 5-10% equity
   - Where: IIM incubators, T-Hub, Startup Oasis
   - Pros: Best terms, credibility
   - Cons: Competitive, takes time

4. **Government Grants**
   - Amount: ₹5-25L
   - Terms: No equity (grant)
   - Where: Startup India Seed Fund, State schemes
   - Pros: Free money
   - Cons: Paperwork, slow process
