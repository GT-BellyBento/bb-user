# Tiffin Provider Licenses & Requirements

This document outlines the licenses and documents required for **Tiffin Service Providers** to register on BellyBento.

---

## Onboarding Requirements

Every tiffin service provider on BellyBento must submit the following:

| Document | Mandatory | Purpose |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| **FSSAI Registration/License** | ✅ Yes | Food safety compliance |
| **Aadhaar Card** | ✅ Yes | Identity verification (KYC) |
| **PAN Card** | ✅ Yes | Tax compliance & payments |
| **Bank Account Details** | ✅ Yes | Payment settlement |
| **Kitchen Photos** | ✅ Yes | Hygiene verification |
| **Address Proof** | ✅ Yes | Service area validation |
| **GST Registration** | Optional | Required if turnover > ₹20 lakh |

## FSSAI Requirements

### Which FSSAI Type Do You Need?

| Provider Type | Annual Turnover | FSSAI Requirement | Annual Fee |
|---------------|-----------------|-------------------|------------|
| **Small Home Kitchen** | < ₹12 lakh | Basic Registration | ₹100 |
| **Medium Scale** | ₹12 - ₹20 lakh | State License | ₹2,000 |
| **Large Scale** | > ₹20 lakh | Central License | ₹5,000 |

> **Note**: Most home-based tiffin providers qualify for **Basic Registration** (₹100/year)

## How to Get FSSAI Registration

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Visit FoSCoS Portal**
   - Go to [foscos.fssai.gov.in](https://foscos.fssai.gov.in)
   - Click on "FBO Registration/License"

2. **Create Account**
   - Register with mobile number and email
   - Verify OTP

3. **Select Registration Type**
   - Choose "Registration" for small operators (< ₹12 lakh turnover)
   - Choose "License" for larger operations

4. **Fill Form A (Registration)**
   - Business name and type
   - Kitchen/premises address
   - Food categories you prepare
   - Owner details

5. **Upload Documents**
   - Aadhaar card
   - Photo of applicant
   - Kitchen photos
   - Address proof

6. **Pay Fee Online**
   - ₹100 for 1 year
   - ₹200 for 2 years
   - ₹500 for 5 years

7. **Receive Registration Number**
   - 14-digit FSSAI number issued
   - Valid for 1-5 years based on payment

### Documents for FSSAI Registration

| Document | Format | Size Limit |
|----------|--------|------------|
| Aadhaar Card | PDF/JPG | 2 MB |
| Passport Photo | JPG | 500 KB |
| Kitchen Photos | JPG | 2 MB each |
| Address Proof | PDF/JPG | 2 MB |

## Provider Responsibilities

Once registered on BellyBento, providers must:

### Food Safety
- ✅ Maintain kitchen hygiene standards
- ✅ Use quality, fresh ingredients
- ✅ Proper food storage (hot/cold as required)
- ✅ Clean cooking equipment daily
- ✅ Wash hands before food preparation

### Packaging
- ✅ Use food-grade containers
- ✅ Seal containers properly
- ✅ Display FSSAI number on packaging
- ✅ Include date of preparation

### Delivery
- ✅ Deliver within 2-3 hours of preparation
- ✅ Use insulated bags for transport
- ✅ Maintain delivery schedule

### Compliance
- ✅ Renew FSSAI before expiry
- ✅ Keep FSSAI certificate displayed in kitchen
- ✅ Respond to customer complaints promptly
- ✅ Inform BellyBento of any changes in operations

## GST Registration (Optional)

### When Required
- Annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh
- Providing interstate services

### How to Register
1. Visit [GST Portal](https://www.gst.gov.in)
2. Fill GST REG-01 form
3. Upload PAN, Aadhaar, bank details
4. Receive GSTIN within 7 days

## Kitchen Hygiene Checklist

BellyBento may verify these during onboarding:

| Area | Requirements |
|------|--------------|
| **Kitchen Floor** | Clean, non-slippery, washable |
| **Cooking Area** | Separate from storage, well-ventilated |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I start without FSSAI?
**No.** FSSAI registration is mandatory before listing on BellyBento. It's required by law for any food business.

### Q: How long does FSSAI registration take?
**7-14 days** for basic registration. License applications may take 30-60 days.

### Q: Can I operate from home?
**Yes.** Home-based tiffin services are allowed. FSSAI basic registration covers home kitchens.

### Q: What if I cook multiple cuisines?
You can register for multiple food categories in a single FSSAI registration.

### Q: Do I need separate registration for delivery?
**No.** Your FSSAI registration covers both preparation and delivery of food you prepare.
