# Customer Monetization Strategy (Platform Fees & Subscriptions)

A practical guide to charging customers on a tiffin marketplace platform.

---

## Introduction

Unlike providers (who get direct business value), customers use the platform simply to find and order tiffins. This makes customer monetization tricky:

- **Too many charges** → Customers leave for direct ordering
- **No charges** → Platform loses a revenue stream

The key is to charge for **convenience and value-added services**, not just for using the platform.

> **Golden Rule:** Customers pay for convenience, speed, and trust – not for the privilege of ordering food.

---

## Types of Customer Charges

### Overview

| Charge Type | What It Is | When to Charge | Typical Amount |
|-------------|-----------|----------------|----------------|
| **Platform Fee** | Small fee per order | Every order | ₹5-₹15 |
| **Delivery Fee** | Charge for delivery | If platform handles delivery | ₹20-₹50 |
| **Convenience Fee** | For special features | Optional services | ₹10-₹30 |
| **Subscription** | Monthly membership | Recurring | ₹99-₹299/month |
| **Surge Pricing** | Peak time premium | High demand periods | +10-20% |

---

## Platform Fee (Service Fee)

### What Is It?

A small fixed fee added to every order for using the platform. This covers:
- Payment processing
- Customer support
- Order management
- Platform maintenance

### Pricing Strategy

| Order Value | Platform Fee | % of Order |
|-------------|--------------|------------|
| ₹50-₹100 | ₹5 | 5-10% |
| ₹100-₹200 | ₹10 | 5-10% |
| ₹200-₹500 | ₹15 | 3-7% |
| ₹500+ | ₹15-₹20 | 3-4% |

### Example Order Breakdown

```
Order Summary:
─────────────────────────────
Tiffin (Lunch)         ₹100
Platform Fee           ₹10
─────────────────────────────
Total                  ₹110
```

### Best Practices

- ✅ Keep it small (under 10% of order value)
- ✅ Show it clearly in checkout (transparency builds trust)
- ✅ Waive for first order (acquisition strategy)
- ✅ Remove for subscription members
- ❌ Don't hide it in item prices

## Delivery Fee

### When Applicable

Only if the platform handles delivery (not provider's own delivery):

| Scenario | Who Delivers | Delivery Fee |
|----------|--------------|--------------|
| Provider delivers | Provider | ₹0 (included in tiffin price) |
| Platform delivery fleet | Platform | ₹20-₹50 |
| Third-party (Dunzo, etc.) | Partner | ₹30-₹60 |

### Distance-Based Pricing

| Distance | Delivery Fee |
|----------|--------------|
| 0-2 km | ₹20 |
| 2-5 km | ₹30 |
| 5-8 km | ₹40 |
| 8+ km | ₹50 or Not serviceable |

### Free Delivery Thresholds

Encourage larger orders:

| Order Value | Delivery Fee |
|-------------|--------------|
| Below ₹150 | ₹30 |
| ₹150-₹300 | ₹15 |
| Above ₹300 | FREE |

### Example

```
Order Summary:
─────────────────────────────
Lunch Tiffin           ₹100
Dinner Tiffin          ₹100
─────────────────────────────
Subtotal               ₹200
Platform Fee           ₹10
Delivery Fee           ₹15  (₹30 - ₹15 discount for ₹200+ order)
─────────────────────────────
Total                  ₹225
```

## Convenience Fee

### What Is It?

Extra charges for special services that add convenience:

| Service | Fee | Description |
|---------|-----|-------------|
| **Express Delivery** | ₹20-₹30 | Delivery within 30 mins instead of 1 hour |
| **Scheduled Delivery** | ₹10 | Choose exact delivery time slot |
| **Special Instructions** | ₹0-₹15 | Custom requests (less spicy, extra roti) |
| **Multiple Addresses** | ₹15/address | Split order to office + home |
| **Cash on Delivery** | ₹10 | If platform prefers digital payments |

### When to Use

- Only for **optional** premium services
- Never for basic ordering functionality
- Customers should be able to avoid these charges

## Customer Subscription Plans

### Why Subscriptions Work

Subscriptions turn occasional users into loyal customers:
- **Predictable revenue** for platform
- **Savings** for regular customers
- **Higher retention** (sunk cost psychology)

### Recommended Plans

#### Free (Default)
**Price:** ₹0/month

| Feature | Included |
|---------|----------|
| Browse providers | ✅ |
| Place orders | ✅ |
| Platform fee | ₹10/order |
| Delivery fee | Standard rates |
| Support | Standard |

**Best for:** Occasional users, first-time customers

### ROI for Customers

**Plus Plan Example (₹99/month):**
```
Assuming 15 orders/month, ₹100 average order:

Without Plus:
- Platform fee: 15 × ₹10 = ₹150
- Delivery: 15 × ₹15 = ₹225
- Total fees: ₹375

With Plus (₹99):
- Platform fee: ₹0
- Delivery (orders above ₹150): ₹0
- 5% discount: 15 × ₹100 × 5% = ₹75 saved
- Total cost: ₹99 subscription

Savings: ₹375 - ₹99 - ₹75 discount = ₹201/month saved ✅
```

## Surge Pricing (Peak Hours)

### When to Apply

During high-demand periods when providers are busy:

| Time | Surge | Reason |
|------|-------|--------|
| 12:00 - 1:30 PM | +10-15% | Lunch rush |
| 7:30 - 9:00 PM | +10-15% | Dinner rush |
| Festivals/Holidays | +15-20% | Limited availability |
| Bad weather | +10% | Delivery challenges |

### How to Implement

```
Order Summary:
─────────────────────────────
Lunch Tiffin           ₹100
Peak Hour Charge       ₹10  (10% surge - high demand)
Platform Fee           ₹10
─────────────────────────────
Total                  ₹120
```

### Best Practices

- ✅ Be transparent ("High demand - ₹10 extra")
- ✅ Show countdown ("Normal pricing in 45 mins")
- ✅ Exempt subscription members from surge
- ❌ Don't surge above 20% (customers will cancel)

## What NOT to Charge

Some fees will drive customers away:

| Bad Idea | Why It Fails |
|----------|--------------|
| **Browsing fee** | Customers will use competitors |
| **Search fee** | Basic functionality must be free |
| **High platform fees (>15%)** | Feels like a rip-off |
| **Cancellation fee (always)** | Sometimes mistakes happen |
| **Hidden fees at checkout** | Destroys trust, high cart abandonment |

> **Rule:** If customers can get it free elsewhere, don't charge for it.

## When to Introduce Customer Charges

### Phased Approach

| Phase | Timeline | Charges |
|-------|----------|---------|
| **Launch** | Month 1-3 | NO fees (grow user base) |
| **Growth** | Month 4-6 | Small platform fee (₹5) |
| **Maturity** | Month 6-12 | Full fees + subscriptions |
| **Scale** | Year 1+ | Optimize based on data |

### Conditions Before Charging

Before introducing platform fees:
- [ ] 500+ active customers
- [ ] Customers ordering 2+ times/month on average
- [ ] Strong retention (60%+ return rate)

Before introducing subscriptions:
- [ ] 1000+ active customers
- [ ] Heavy users identifiable (15+ orders/month)
- [ ] Clear value proposition ready

## Key Takeaways

### Pricing Principles

- ✅ **Start free, add fees gradually** – Build habit first, monetize later
- ✅ **Keep fees small and transparent** – Under 10% of order value
- ✅ **Offer ways to avoid fees** – Subscriptions, order thresholds
- ✅ **Charge for convenience, not access** – Basic features stay free
- ✅ **Test pricing with A/B tests** – Different fees for different cohorts

### Quick Reference

| Charge Type | Recommended Amount | When to Start |
|-------------|-------------------|---------------|
| Platform Fee | ₹5-₹15/order | After 500 customers |
| Delivery Fee | ₹20-₹50 | If platform handles delivery |
| Subscription | ₹99-₹199/month | After 1000 customers |

### Customer Fee vs Provider Commission

| Revenue Source | From Whom | Amount | % of Revenue |
|----------------|-----------|--------|--------------|
| Commission | Providers | 5-10% | 60-70% |
| Platform Fee | Customers | ₹5-₹15 | 15-20% |
| Subscriptions | Both | ₹99-₹999 | 10-20% |

### Do's and Don'ts

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|-------|---------|
| Be transparent about fees | Hide fees until checkout |
| Offer subscription to avoid fees | Make fees unavoidable |
| Waive fees for first orders | Charge from day 1 |
| Keep platform fee under ₹15 | Charge more than 15% |
| Reward loyalty | Treat all customers the same |

## Glossary

- **Platform Fee** – Fixed charge for using the ordering platform
- **Delivery Fee** – Charge for transporting the order
- **Convenience Fee** – Extra charge for premium/optional services
- **Surge Pricing** – Dynamic pricing during high-demand periods
- **Subscription** – Monthly membership with bundled benefits
- **Free Delivery Threshold** – Minimum order value for free delivery
