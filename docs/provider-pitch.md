# Provider Pitch & Early Bird Incentives

A simple, layman-friendly guide for pitching to home cooks and tiffin providers.

---

## Early Bird Incentive Structure

### Provider Incentives

| When They Join | Free Period (No Commission) |
|----------------|----------------------------|
| **Waitlist (before launch)** | 2 months FREE |
| **After launch** | 1 month FREE |
| **After Month 3** | No free period |

### Customer Incentives

| When They Join | Free Period (No Fees) |
|----------------|----------------------|
| **Waitlist (before launch)** | 3 months FREE |
| **After launch** | 1 month FREE |
| **After Month 3** | No free period |

### Why This Works

1. **Creates urgency** - "Join waitlist NOW to get 2 months free"
2. **Rewards early believers** - They took a chance on you
3. **Word of mouth** - Early users tell others "I got 2 months free, you only get 1!"
4. **Easy to track** - Waitlist date = proof of early signup

**Hindi-English:**
```

Humara platform hai - BellyBento. 

Hum aapko customers laake denge - jo log apne ghar se door rehte hain aur ghar ka khana miss karte hain.

Aap khana banao, hum customer laayenge. 
Aap price rakho, aap decide karo kitne tiffin banana hai.
Koi pressure nahi.

Aur abhi join karoge to 2 mahine tak koi charge nahi - jo kamao sab aapka."

**English:**
```
"You cook at home, right? And you cook really well.

We have a platform called BellyBento.

We bring customers to you - people who live away from home and miss home-cooked food.

You cook, we bring customers.
You set the price, you decide how many tiffins to make.
No pressure.

And if you join now, 2 months no charges - whatever you earn is 100% yours."
```

```

- Waitlist mein join karo to 2 mahine tak koi charge nahi

Hum wo khane waale laate hain. Aap bas wahi karo jo aap best karte ho - khana banana.

Interested ho? Main abhi aapka number le leta hoon, jab launch hoga seedha aapko batayenge.

## Common Questions & Answers (FAQ)

### Q: Mujhe phone/app chalana nahi aata
```
"Koi baat nahi. Hum aapko sikhayenge. Bahut simple hai - WhatsApp chalana aata hai? Bas utna hi easy hai. Aur problem ho to hum hain na."
```

### Q: Kitna commission lete ho?
```
"Abhi waitlist join karoge to 2 mahine zero. Uske baad bhi sirf ₹10-15 per order. Aap ₹100 ka tiffin becho, ₹85-90 aapka."
```

### Q: Customer nahi aaye to?
```
"Customer laana humara kaam hai. Aap sirf accha khana banao. Humari team marketing karti hai, customers dhundhti hai."
```

### Q: License wagera chahiye?
```
"Abhi starting mein nahi chahiye. Jab aapka business badhe, hum help karenge FSSAI license lene mein. Woh bhi free mein guidance denge."
```

### Q: Daily banana padega?
```
"Nahi nahi. Aap decide karo - Monday off chahiye? Le lo. Sirf lunch banana hai? Thik hai. Sirf 5 tiffin? Thik hai. Full control aapka."
```

### Q: Payment kaise milega?
```
"Weekly ya monthly - jo aapko suit kare. Seedha aapke bank account mein. Koi cash handling nahi."
```

### Q: Competition zyada hai to?
```
"Acchi baat hai! Competition matlab demand hai. Aur hum aapko apne area ke customers dikhayenge. Jo aapke paas se order karna chahein."
```
