## 1. Pitch Deck

### Purpose
A 10-15 slide presentation for investor meetings.

### Recommended Structure

| Slide | Content |
|-------|---------|
| 1. Title | BellyBento logo, tagline, contact |
| 2. Problem | Pain points for customers & providers |
| 3. Solution | How BellyBento solves it |
| 4. Market Size | TAM, SAM, SOM analysis |
| 5. Product | Screenshots, demo, features |
| 6. Business Model | How we make money |
| 7. Traction | Waitlist signups, pilot data |
| 8. Competition | Competitive landscape & differentiation |
| 9. Go-to-Market | Launch strategy, growth plan |
| 10. Team | Founder backgrounds |
| 11. Financials | Revenue projections, unit economics |
| 12. Ask | Funding amount, use of funds |
| 13. Contact | How to reach you |

### Tips
- Keep it visual, minimal text
- Use real data from waitlist
- Include customer/provider testimonials if available
- Show traction metrics prominently

## 3. Market Research

### Purpose
Data-driven analysis of the market opportunity.

### Key Metrics to Research

#### Market Size

| Metric | Description | How to Calculate |
|--------|-------------|------------------|
| **TAM** (Total Addressable Market) | Total food delivery market in India | Industry reports |
| **SAM** (Serviceable Addressable Market) | Tiffin/home food segment | TAM × Tiffin % share |
| **SOM** (Serviceable Obtainable Market) | Realistic capture in 3-5 years | SAM × Target % |

#### Industry Data Points
- India food delivery market size (2026)
- Growth rate (CAGR)
- Number of working professionals/students in target cities
- Average monthly food expense
- Tiffin service adoption rate

#### Competitor Analysis

| Competitor | Model | Cities | Funding | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|------------|-------|--------|---------|-----------|------------|
| HomeFoodi | Home chefs + Tiffin + Catering | 16 | Unknown | Wide presence | Fragmented focus |
| Swiggy Daily | Restaurant meals | Major metros | Well-funded | Brand recognition | Not home-cooked |
| Local tiffin services | Offline | Local | None | Trust | No tech, limited reach |

#### Target Customer Research
- Demographics (age, occupation, location)
- Pain points (survey data)
- Willingness to pay
- Feature preferences

## 5. Founder Agreement

### Purpose
Legal agreement between co-founders (if multiple founders).

### Key Clauses

| Clause | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Equity Split** | Percentage ownership of each founder |
| **Vesting Schedule** | Typically 4 years with 1-year cliff |
| **Roles & Responsibilities** | Who handles what |
| **Decision Making** | Voting rights, major decisions |
| **IP Assignment** | All IP belongs to company |
| **Full-time Commitment** | Dedication expectations |
| **Exit Provisions** | What happens if founder leaves |
| **Non-Compete** | Restrictions on competing business |
| **Dispute Resolution** | Arbitration, mediation process |

### Recommended Vesting
- **Standard**: 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff
- **Example**: 25% vests after 1 year, then monthly for next 3 years

### Get Legal Help
Hire a startup lawyer to draft this. Approximate cost: ₹15,000 - ₹50,000

- AngelList India
- LetsVenture

- Startup India portal

- VC firm websites (Sequoia Surge, Blume, etc.)

### Warm Introductions
- Ask existing connections
- Attend startup events
- Use accelerator networks

### Cold Outreach
- Research investor portfolio
- Personalize your message
- Lead with traction/metrics
- Keep email under 200 words
